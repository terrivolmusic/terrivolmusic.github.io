11 — FAQ
Before you ask
How does the collar work?

The padded scarf-collar has snap buttons. Snap it up to the chin for a hood-like fit, or snap it down over the shoulders like a scarf. Same collar, two completely different silhouettes.

What is it made of?

High-gloss quilted polyester, fully padded. The quilt channels are structural — they hold warmth and shape. 700 g total weight.

How do I choose my size?

Sizes run S, M, L in an oversized, cropped cut. The model wears M. Full garment measurements are in section 09. The cut is roomy by design — if you are between sizes and want it less voluminous, size down.

Is it warm?

Yes. The quilted polyester traps air efficiently. At 700 g it's substantial but not heavy — designed for night temperatures in motion.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes — free tracked shipping worldwide. Processing takes 2–3 business days, delivery 8–15 days after that.

Can I return it?

Yes. 7-day unconditional returns from delivery. Send it back in the condition you received it.
