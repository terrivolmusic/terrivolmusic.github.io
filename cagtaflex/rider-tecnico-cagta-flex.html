This document outlines the technical requirements for a Cagta Flex live performance featuring DJ G.Promode and MPC Live II integration. Please ensure all listed equipment is available at soundcheck time. Any substitutions must be approved by the Tour Manager prior to arrival.

Audio & Monitoring

Input Sources (Artist Provided)
  • MPC Live II Stereo L/R Output (TRS/XLR)
  • Pioneer CDJ-3000 x2 Digital Link + Analog Aux
  • Laptop Backup Ableton Live Suite (Stereo Out)
  • Vocals Shure Beta 58A (Wireless Preferred)
Front of House (FOH)
  • Channels Needed: Min. 8 Ch.
  • Mic Inputs: 2x XLR Female (Phantom Power OFF)
  • Line Inputs: 4x TRS Balanced (for MPC/DJ/Backup)
  • FX Sends: 2x Independent Reverb/Delay buses
  • Main Outs: XLR Balanced to PA System
Stage Monitors
  • Type: Wedge Monitors (Active)
  • Quantity: 2 Units Min.
  • Mix Requirement: Vocal Heavy + Instrumental Bed
  • IEM Option: Shure PSM300 (Freq scan required)

Stage Plot Layout

MC(VOCALS)
↑ Center Stage
MPC(BEATS)

DJ(CONSOLA)

AUDIENCE / FOH ▲
Note: Minimum stage depth of 4 meters required behind front edge to accommodate cable runs and safe movement during interactive beatmaking segments. Screen/VJ feed preferred center-rear or side-flank depending on venue geometry.

Power & Lighting Basics

Electrical
  • Circuits: 3 Dedicated Circuits (Clean Power)
  • Outlets: IEC C13 / Schuko (depending on region)
  • Grounding: Strictly isolated ground for audio gear
Lighting Mood
  • Key Light: Warm White Spot on MC/MPC area
  • Accent: Cyan/Magenta Wash (Brand Colors)
  • Haze: Low density haze recommended for beams
